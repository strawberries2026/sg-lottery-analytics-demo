About

SG Lottery Analytics Pro is a free, independently developed analysis tool. It provides random selections, historical frequency analysis, CSV import/export, and optional retrieval of third-party 4D and TOTO results.

Historical feed: 4dlivetoday.com (CC BY 4.0 attribution). Results are not independently verified and coverage may be incomplete. Big Sweep has no connected historical feed.

Records are stored locally in your browser, not synced across devices. Export a CSV backup before clearing browser data. Offline access depends on whether the app has been cached successfully.

Number selections are not predictions. The app does not sell tickets or improve the probability of winning. Confirm official results and rules with Singapore Pools.

No paid hosting or external AI service is required.
